1. About us

Artisan Oven is a student-run pizza enterprise operating from school premises in Marlow. Orders are placed weekly through this website and collected at lunchtime on the service date.

2. Ordering

  • Orders must be placed before the published closing time (typically Sunday 9 pm).
  • Placing an order constitutes acceptance of these terms.
  • We reserve the right to decline or cancel orders at our discretion.
  • Submitting duplicate orders for the same service date is not permitted.

3. Payment

  • Payment is due at or before collection. We accept cash and online payment via PayPal or bank transfer.
  • Where a discount code is applied, the discounted price is binding.
  • Failure to pay may result in your order being declined in future sessions.

4. Allergens & dietary requirements

Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs, and other common allergens. We cannot guarantee a fully allergen-free environment. If you have a severe allergy, please contact us before ordering. It is your responsibility to provide accurate allergen information in your order notes.

5. Cancellations & refunds

  • Orders may be cancelled up to 24 hours before the service date by contacting us directly.
  • Refunds for online payments will be processed within 5 business days.
  • We are not liable for orders uncollected by the customer.

6. Privacy

We collect your name and email address solely to fulfil your order and send confirmation messages. We do not share your data with third parties. WhatsApp notifications are only sent if you opt in. Email addresses are stored securely and used only for order-related communications.

7. Contact

For any queries, contact us at louis@benne.co.uk or via WhatsApp.
